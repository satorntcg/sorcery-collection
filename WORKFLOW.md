## Streamlined Workflow

### Session 1 — Film (any day)

1. Open 12 packs, record exceptional / epic / unique / foil pulls on iPhone
2. ~~Manual copy~~ → iPhone auto-syncs to PC via iCloud or Windows robocopy

**Pack battle:** Same process, just 2 packs. Treat as a lighter version of the same checklist — not a separate workflow.

---

### Session 2 — Edit (same day or next day)

> **Key change:** Log cards in the app *before* opening Premiere. The app becomes the price source of truth — no need to maintain the Google Sheet separately during editing.

3. Import pulls into SatornTCG app (spreadsheet upload or manual entry) — prices auto-populate
   - App now has P&L, big hits list (≥$10), and listing suggestions ready before you touch Premiere
4. Open Premiere, drop 12 clips into template, edit 12-pack video using prices from app
5. Cut big hit clips (1–3) from timeline:
   - Export **with** price/text overlay → YouTube short
   - Toggle overlay off, export again → Reddit clean version
   - **Zero extra editing — two exports only**
6. Create YouTube opening in SatornTCG app — confirms P&L, surfaces big hits
7. **[To build]** Click "Generate YouTube copy" in app → Claude drafts title, description, and short titles from actual P&L + best pull + set name. Review and approve.
8. Create thumbnail in GIMP, review with Claude

---

### Session 3 — Upload & Distribute (same session as edit)

9. Upload 12-pack video → schedule Friday. Upload pack battle → schedule Saturday.
   - Paste titles + descriptions from app — no rewriting
10. Upload big hit shorts → schedule Sunday + Wed/Thu fill slots
    - Review short titles with Claude before scheduling
11. Update YouTube URLs in SatornTCG app for opening + biggest short
12. **[To build]** App surfaces eBay listing suggestions from this box's big hits — photograph cards and create listings in same session while cards are already out
13. Post clean short to Reddit with Claude-drafted caption (no extra edit — already exported in step 5)

---

### Monday (recurring — no filming needed)

- **[To build]** Top movers video + Reddit post
  - Pull weekly movers data from SatornTCG app (Weekly Movers view)
  - App generates video script + Reddit caption
  - Record as screen capture with voiceover, or talk-to-camera over the data
  - Post Reddit weekly movers write-up

---

### Daily (2 min)

- Check Alerts tab in app — dismiss resolved alerts, adjust any flagged eBay listings
- Post in daily Sorcery Reddit threads (Claude can help draft variety)

## Features to Build in SatornTCG App

### Priority 1 — Generate YouTube copy
- Button on the YouTube opening summary screen
- Inputs: P&L, best pull card name + value, set name, pack count
- Output: ready-to-paste title, description, and short title(s)
- Claude drafts, you approve — no manual writing

### Priority 2 — Top movers Monday workflow
- "Generate Monday content" button on Weekly Movers view
- Output: video script (talk-to-camera or voiceover format) + Reddit caption
- Recurring every Monday — zero filming required

### Priority 3 — Big hit → eBay listing linkage
- When a card is flagged as a big hit in an opening (≥$10), auto-surface it on the listing suggestions page
- Connection: "I just pulled this on camera" → "list it today while it's relevant"

## Key Principles

- **App is source of truth** — log cards before editing, not during
- **One pull, three actions** — a big hit card triggers a short + listing suggestion + Reddit draft automatically
- **Two exports, not two edits** — overlay on for YouTube, overlay off for Reddit, done in Premiere
- **Pack battle is not a separate workflow** — same checklist, fewer packs
- **Monday content is free** — top movers requires no filming, just data you already have
